Steply privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Steply is a visual routine app for parents and carers. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. Short version: everything you make in Steply stays on your phone. We do not collect it, we cannot see it, and there are no ads and no tracking. The only thing that can ever leave the app is a problem report that you write and choose to send.

Who we are

NISICO TECHNOLOGIES SRL, Romania (registered office Strada Stupca 22-32, Sector 6, 061042 Bucharest, Romania, company number J40/4720/16.03.2021, VAT RO43922705). Contact: support@nisico.eu.

Steply is for adults

Steply is a tool for parents and carers. It is not directed to children. A child uses Steply only on a phone or tablet set up by an adult, never creates an account, and never gives us any information.

What stays on your device

Routines, steps, pictures you add or take, your child's first name, reminders and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) is on, your phone may include Steply's data in that backup under Apple's or Google's terms.

What we collect

Nothing, unless you send us a problem report. This version of Steply has no account, no purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting service and no advertising.

Problem reports you send

In Steply you can fill in Report a problem (More > Help and report a problem) and tap Send. Nothing is sent unless you do this, and you see everything before it leaves your phone. A report holds:

  • the name you type and what you write;
  • a short technical log: the last screens you used, the app and phone system version, and recent errors, with no names and no photos.

Reports are received by NISICO TECHNOLOGIES SRL on servers of our processor Supabase in Frankfurt (EU) and deleted after 30 days. The server also keeps a keyed hash of your IP address, kept for about one hour to stop abuse, then deleted; the IP address itself is not stored. We use reports only to fix problems in Steply and to answer you. Legal basis: your consent (you choose to send each report) and our legitimate interest in keeping Steply working.

If you write to us by email, we use what you send only to answer you, and we delete it when the conversation is over unless the law requires us to keep it.

Backup files

A backup file made with More > Export and import leaves your phone only when you share it yourself. It is locked with your passphrase; we never receive it.

Permissions

Photos and camera: only when you choose a picture for a step. Notifications: only for the reminders you set. You can turn either off in your phone's settings.

What we never do

No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or sharing of data, no profiling.

Keeping and deleting

Your data stays on your device until you delete it: in the app (Parent area > Settings) or by deleting the app. See Delete your data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR and US state privacy laws (including the CCPA/CPRA) you may ask us for access, correction or deletion of any personal data we hold about you, and you may object or complain to a data protection authority. In Romania this is ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro). In most cases we hold nothing about you except problem reports you sent in the last 30 days and emails you sent us. To ask us to delete a report sooner, write to support@nisico.eu with the name you used in it.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, backup or purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released and say so in the app.

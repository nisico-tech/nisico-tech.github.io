Steply privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Steply is a visual routine app for parents and other grown-ups who care for a child. A grown-up builds picture routines and a child follows them one card at a time. This policy says what Steply uses, why, who else is involved, how long we keep it and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • Steply works without an account. Without one, your routines, photos, voice recordings and your child's progress stay in Steply's storage on your phone and are not uploaded to us.
  • An account is optional. With one, your child's first name, routines and progress are kept on our server in Frankfurt, Germany, so they are on all your family's phones. Before anything about your child is uploaded, we ask you to confirm that you are the child's parent or guardian and that you agree.
  • Your family's content is encrypted on your phone before it is synced, so our server cannot read it. To keep phones in step, the server still sees random ids, dates and times, and simple settings.
  • Photos and voice recordings are uploaded only if you turn on backup, and they are encrypted on your phone first. A routine link you create includes that routine's photos unless you turn them off.
  • No usage statistics, no crash reporting and no install id are sent by this version of Steply.
  • Your child never has an account and is never asked for anything.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites, no profiling, no AI training on your content.
  • You can delete your account and your family's data on our server from inside the app at any time.

Who uses Steply

Steply is for parents, legal guardians and other grown-ups who look after a child with a parent's agreement. Accounts, purchases, settings, sharing and deletion sit behind a "Grown-ups only" check. The person who creates a family account must be the child's parent or legal guardian, or act with their permission. Other grown-ups join only when invited.

A child uses Steply only in Child mode, on a phone a grown-up has set up. Child mode shows the routine cards and a Done button. It has no account, sign-up, chat, links, purchases, ads or settings, and never asks the child to type or say anything.

What stays on your phone

Your child's first name or nickname; routines, steps, times, weekdays, Plan B cards and reminders; photos you take or pick for a step (saved without their location and camera data) and voice recordings you make; the record of which steps were done and stars; your settings and PIN.

Spoken step labels use your phone's own speech engine or a voice built into the app; no cloud voice service is used. Reminders are notifications created on the phone; we use no push service. On Android, Steply's data is excluded from Google's cloud backup. Steply never asks for your child's age, birth date, diagnosis, school, location or contacts, and has no advertising identifier.

With an account (optional)

You can sign up with an email address and password (sign-in then also asks for a code we email you), with a one-time email code, or with Google. With an account we also process:

  • your email address, sign-in method, a password hash (email sign-up only) and sign-in times, to run and secure your account;
  • your confirmation that you are the child's parent or guardian, the versions of this policy and the Terms you accepted, and when;
  • your child's first name or nickname, routines, steps, Plan B cards and reminders, so they are the same on your family's phones;
  • the record of steps done and stars, created on your phone in Child mode, so every family phone shows the same progress;
  • other grown-ups in your family (for example a partner, grandparent or carer), who join with a link or an 8-character code and without giving us an email: a random member id, their role, when they joined and their phone's sign-in session;
  • your plan and purchase status from Google Play, through RevenueCat;
  • IP addresses and technical request data in server logs, for security and troubleshooting.

End-to-end encryption. Before your child's name, routine and step words, pictures and their references, schedules, Plan B cards, rewards and help notes leave your phone, Steply encrypts them (AES-GCM, 256-bit) with your family's key, which is kept only on your family's phones. Our server stores only the encrypted values, so neither we nor our hosting provider can read them. To keep phones in step, the server does see random ids, which family and phone a change came from, when it was made, whether a field is empty, the order of steps, durations and other numbers, on/off settings and settings picked from a fixed list, and when a routine was started and finished.

A grown-up who joins gets the family key from your phone, by QR code or by typing it. If you lose every phone that holds the key, we cannot open your family's synced data for you. When you remove a grown-up, their phone stops syncing at once, but what is already on it stays there and it still holds the family key; we do not yet change the key when someone is removed.

From Google, if you sign in with it, we receive your email address and the name and picture on that profile. We remove the name and picture as soon as your account is created and never show them.

Photo and voice backup (separate choice)

Photos and voice recordings are not uploaded just because you have an account. They are uploaded only after you tap "Turn on backup". Each file is encrypted on your phone first (AES-GCM, 256-bit) with a key kept in your family's phones' secure storage. Our server stores the encrypted files and is designed never to receive that key. You can remove the backup at any time with "Delete from cloud", and it is deleted with your account. If you lose every phone that holds the key, we cannot recover the files.

Sharing a routine

  • Exported file. A routine you export is a file protected by a passphrase you choose. You decide where it goes; once sent, it is outside our control.
  • Routine links. You can create a link so another grown-up can see a routine. Steply first shows what the link will contain: the routine's words and, unless you turn them off, its photos. Our server keeps an encrypted copy. The link opens only with a separate code that Steply gives you and that our server never receives; after 5 wrong codes the link stops. Anyone with both the link and the code can see the routine until the link ends, even if it is forwarded. A link ends after 7 days, or sooner if you stop it. If you are signed in, our server records which family made it, so deleting your account or family stops all your family's links. Links open on steply.nisico.eu, hosted by GitHub, which sees visitors' IP addresses in its logs. The link page loads nothing from other companies and is not indexed by search engines.
  • Report a link. Anyone who sees a link that should not exist can report it from the link page or at support@nisico.eu, and we stop it without opening it. A report holds the link's id, the reason, anything the reporter types and, only if they give it, an email for our answer; we keep it 12 months.

Please share only what you are comfortable with, and not photos of other people's children without their parents' agreement. Printing uses your phone's own print service; nothing printed is sent to us.

Steply Plus and the emails we send

Steply Plus is bought through Google Play, which takes the payment; we never see your card. RevenueCat manages the subscription for us and receives a random user id (linked to your account id), the purchase receipt and plan status, never anything about your child. Plus needs an account with an email address.

  • Before a charge we email you 2 days before a free trial turns into a paid plan, about 30 days and again 2 days before each yearly renewal, and at least 30 days before a price change. Google sends its own notices too.
  • Other service emails (account only): sign-in codes, the confirmation of your parent consent, security notices, notices before we delete an inactive account, and important changes to the Terms or this policy. We send no marketing emails.

Emails are sent for us by Resend. They never mention autism or any health condition.

Problem reports

From Settings > Support you can send us a problem report. You see the full text before you tap Send. It holds what you type (a name you choose and what happened) and a short diagnostic (app version, device model, OS version, recent error codes) with personal content removed; no photos. It goes to our server in Frankfurt and we keep it 30 days. To prevent abuse we keep a salted hash of your IP address for about one hour.

App updates

Steply can download small updates of its own code from Expo's update service, which sees your IP address and the app version when it checks.

No usage statistics

This version of Steply sends no usage statistics, no crash reports and no install id or other identifier for analytics. If a later version asks to send optional statistics, it will ask you first and this policy will say so before that version reaches you.

About health. Steply does not ask for health information, and you should not type it into routine or step names. Because many families who use Steply have an autistic child, a routine, or the fact that a family uses Steply, could reveal something about a child's health. That is why we ask for your explicit consent, in its own box when you create an account, separately from the Terms. You can say no and keep using Steply without an account. You withdraw it in Settings > What is shared > Your consents, which leads to Delete account: we then delete the account and the family's data on our server, and Steply keeps working on your phone.

How we protect it

Synced data is stored in the EU, encrypted in transit and at rest. Every server table is locked so a family can reach only its own rows. Family content, backups and routine links are encrypted on your phone before upload, as described above. Exported files are locked with your passphrase. Only people who need it can reach production systems, with two-factor sign-in. Our security has not been audited by an independent firm yet. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

This section is for parents and is our notice under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws.

  • What we collect about a child: only what a family grown-up enters and what Child mode records: a first name or nickname, routines, photos and voice recordings you add (which may show or sound like your child), and the record of steps done. Without an account all of it stays on your phone.
  • Your consent first. Before anything about your child is uploaded, Steply asks you, signed in with your email address, to confirm that you are the child's parent or legal guardian and that you agree. We keep a record of it, and about a day later we email you a confirmation that says how to withdraw. Until you agree, your child's data stays on your phone.
  • Withdrawing. In Settings > What is shared > Your consents, or by replying to that email: uploads stop at once and Steply keeps working on your phone.
  • How we use it: only to show, sync and back up your routines. Never for advertising, profiling, AI training or anything else. Nothing is sent about your child for statistics.
  • Who else sees it: only the service providers above, the grown-ups you add to your family, and people you send a routine link and its code.
  • Your choices: you can review, correct and delete your child's information in the app, stop further collection (sign out or stop syncing), or ask us at support@nisico.eu. Using Steply never depends on giving more than it needs.

If you think a child has given us information without a parent, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights and choices

You can ask us to see or get a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect what we did before). Most of this you can do yourself in the app: Settings > Account, export, "Delete from cloud" and "Delete account". Without an account, deleting the app or clearing its storage deletes everything. For anything else, write to support@nisico.eu; we may need to confirm it is you. We answer within one month (up to two more months for complex requests, and we tell you), or within 45 days where a US state law sets that. It is free unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

EU users can complain to their data protection authority; ours is the Romanian ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro). We do not sell or "share" personal information as California defines it, and do not use it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of; a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal changes nothing because we already treat everyone as opted out. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer.

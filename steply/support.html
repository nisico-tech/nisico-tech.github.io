Common questions

How do I open the parent area? Tap "Parent" and solve the short grown-up check.

My child tapped Done by mistake. Tap "Go back" right after Done; it stays on the screen for a few seconds.

Where is my data? On your device only. Steply has no ads and no tracking.

How do I delete everything? See Delete your data.

Something does not work. In the parent area open Settings > Support and share the problem report with us. You can read it before you send it.

Steply is a tool for families. It does not give medical advice and does not replace a therapist or doctor.

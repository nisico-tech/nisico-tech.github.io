Delete your Steply data

Steply keeps your routines, pictures and your child's first name on your device only. We do not hold a copy.

Delete it in the app

Delete the Steply app from your phone or tablet. This removes all of its data from that device. If your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) is on, also remove Steply from that backup in your phone's settings.

Delete what you sent us

If you emailed us or sent a problem report, write to support@nisico.eu with the subject "Delete my Steply data". We delete those messages within 30 days and confirm by email.

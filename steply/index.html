Inside Steply

What Steply does

Picture schedules for every routine

Morning, school, bedtime: picture routines on one calm daily calendar. Hold a card and drag it to a new time.

One step at a time

Your child sees one big picture and one Done button. Step names can be read aloud.

First / Then in seconds

Pick two pictures, tap Show, and your child sees what happens now and what comes next.

A Plan B for hard days

Rain, a closed place, a sick day: your child sees what happens instead. Swap one card or use a ready Plan B; your usual routines are kept.

How it works

  1. 1

    Set up the routine

    Start from a ready-made routine for home, school or outings and add photos of your child's own things. Change any word or picture.

  2. 2

    Show one step at a time

    Hand over the screen. Child mode shows one big step and a Done button; a parent lock keeps everything else closed.

  3. 3

    Adjust when plans change

    Swap a card, show a First / Then, or pick a ready Plan B. Your child sees what happens instead.

What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • Not therapy, a diagnosis or a treatment plan
  • No ads and no social feed
  • No streaks, scores or red marks for your child
  • No sudden sounds or flashing on your child's screen
  • Not a replacement for your child's therapist or teacher

Questions

Who is Steply for?

Parents of autistic children ages 3 to 7, starting with routines at home. No diagnosis is needed: any family that uses picture schedules can use it.

Does my child need an account or their own tablet?

No. Your child uses child mode on your phone or tablet, and a parent lock keeps settings closed. You do not need an account to start.

What happens when the plan changes?

Swap one card for another, show a single Now card, or pick a ready Plan B such as a rainy day or a place that is closed. Your child sees what happens instead. On a day that falls apart, Reset the day lets you start again calmly, and your routines are kept.

Which phones and tablets will it work on?

Steply is being built for iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. It runs on the device you already have; no special tablet is needed.

Will it cost money?

There will be a free version for everyday routines, with no ads. An optional paid plan with extras is planned, and you will always see the price before you pay anything.

Can I print the pictures?

Yes. You can print picture cards and boards for the fridge, the backpack or a day without screens.

Is my child's data private?

Your child does not need an account, and Steply works without one for you too. Photos you add stay on your phone. There are no ads and we never sell data. If you join the waitlist, we keep only your email, with our form provider, to send the launch email.

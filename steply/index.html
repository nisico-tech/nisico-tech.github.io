What Steply does

First / Then in seconds

Pick two pictures, tap once, and your child sees what happens now and what comes next.

Changes, calmly

When the plan changes, show the new step with a clear, gentle swap instead of a surprise.

Private by default

Routines live on your phone. No ads, and no account needed to start.

Home routines first

Morning, bedtime, bath, leaving the house: the moments that are hard every day.

How it works

  1. 1

    Choose a moment

    Start from a ready routine or two pictures.

  2. 2

    Hand over the screen

    The child view shows one step at a time, large and simple.

  3. 3

    Tick it off together

    Each finished step moves out of the way.

What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • Not a therapy or a diagnosis tool
  • No ads, no social feed
  • No streaks or scores for your child

Questions

Who is Steply for?

Parents of autistic children aged about 3 to 7, starting with routines at home. Many other families use visual schedules too.

Does my child need an account?

No. The child sees a simple screen on your device.

Is my data shared?

No. Routines stay on your phone; we do not sell data or show ads.

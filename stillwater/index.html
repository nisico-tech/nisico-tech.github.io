Inside Stillwater

What Stillwater does

Help in the hard minute

Tap “I need help now”: name what is going on, read your own reason, and ride the urge out with a short breathing exercise. Works offline.

A pause where it starts

In the hours you choose, the apps where scrolling turns into porn open on a calm pause, with an adult-content filter. It uses Apple's Screen Time: it slows you down, it cannot lock you out, and we say so.

One hard night doesn't erase your progress

Days on track this month instead of a streak that falls to zero. A slip gets a short look at what came before, not a lecture.

Private and discreet

No account, no ads, no data sold. Your check-ins and notes stay on your phone, and the app has a neutral name on your Home Screen.

What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • No explicit content, ever
  • No streak that resets to zero, no shame, no leaderboards
  • No screenshots or browsing history sent to anyone
  • No fake countdowns, no hidden renewals
  • Not therapy or a medical treatment

Questions

Will it cost money?

The core will be free: help when an urge hits, slip support and the basic night pause. A planned paid plan will add the full lesson program and stronger rules. Whatever we charge, the price and renewal will be stated plainly, you will get a reminder before any charge, and you can cancel in your App Store settings.

Will anyone know I use it?

It is designed to be discreet: a neutral app name on your Home Screen, no account, and your check-ins and notes stay on your phone. If you choose to add an ally, they only see whether your pause is on, off or not confirmed, never sites, apps or screenshots.

Can an app really block porn on an iPhone?

Partly. Through Apple's Screen Time, Stillwater will pause the apps you pick and filter adult websites in the hours you set. iPhone lets you switch Screen Time off in Settings, so no app can honestly promise to be unbreakable. We will tell you what is on and what we cannot confirm, rather than pretend.

I'm worried porn affects my sex life or dating. Is it for me?

Yes. One of the starting paths is for exactly that: short lessons on what porn does and does not do to sex, with sources, and no reboot myths. If you have physical symptoms such as erection problems, please also see a doctor; they can have medical causes.

Is Stillwater religious?

Not by default. It is built for anyone who wants to stop, for their own reasons. If faith is one of them, you will be able to choose a faith path at setup, and it stays as private as everything else.

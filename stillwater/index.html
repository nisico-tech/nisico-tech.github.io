One night, five moments

Every screen has one main button, and it changes with the time of night. You never dig for help.

What it does

  • ☾

    Hard hours

    Pick when the loop usually wins (11 PM to 7 AM by default). In those hours the apps you choose open on a calm pause screen.

  • ⛨

    Adult-site filter

    Adult sites are filtered in every browser through a local VPN that only checks website names. A normal site blocked by mistake goes on your allowed list.

  • 🔒

    Until morning

    During hard hours, turning protection down waits until morning. Turning it up works at once.

  • 🔑

    An ally holds the key

    Give one person a 6-digit code. Ending the night early needs it. They get only the code, never your activity, and need no app.

  • ✉

    Last night, in one line

    In the morning you can send your ally one line built from the phone's own checks. A missed check is shown, never hidden.

  • 🙏

    Faith path, if you want it

    Turn it on and the urge screen adds a verse and a one-minute prayer. Off by default, private like everything else.

Questions

Is Stillwater free?

The protection is free for good: hard hours, apps to pause, the adult-site filter, Until morning, urge help, the ally code, check-ins and the first three lessons. The full 21-day Reset is one purchase of $24.99 (US price), with the faith track included when the Faith path is on, plus the after-day-21 plan (a relapse plan and a monthly check-up). There is no subscription.

Why Android first?

Android lets an app pause other apps and filter sites in a way we can test well today. The iPhone version uses Apple's Screen Time and comes after the Android app is in the store.

Can it really stop me?

It slows you down, and it is honest about the limits. On Android, someone who force-stops the app or turns off its Accessibility permission can switch it off. Stillwater checks itself during the night and the next time you open it, and it shows a missed check instead of hiding it.

What does my ally see?

Only the 6-digit code you send them. No apps, no sites, no times. If you choose to, you can also send them a one-line morning report with the check times; you see the exact text before you send it.

Why does it need Accessibility and a VPN?

Accessibility lets Stillwater see which app opens, so it can pause the ones you picked during your hard hours. It reads nothing on screen. The VPN is local: it only sends website names to Cloudflare for Families, which answers that adult sites don't exist. Your other traffic never goes through it.

Is it religious?

Not by default. It is built for anyone who wants their nights back. If faith matters to you, turn on the Faith path for a verse and a one-minute prayer on the urge screen.

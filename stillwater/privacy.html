Stillwater privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Stillwater is a self-help app for adults who want to stop or cut down porn, mostly at night. It has no account and no server of ours that receives your data. This policy covers the Android app and this website.

The short version

  • What you enter and what the app records stays on your phone: your hard hours, the apps you pick, your reason, your voice note, your morning check-ins, nights kept, lesson progress, your ally code and the protection checks.
  • No account, no email, no name, no ads, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no selling of data.
  • Three things can leave the phone, each explained below: website names checked by the adult-site filter, the purchase record if you buy the Reset, and messages you choose to send yourself.

What stays on your phone

Everything above is stored in the app's private storage. Your voice note is an audio file in that storage. The app's data is excluded from Android's cloud backup, so uninstalling Stillwater deletes all of it and we keep no copy.

Permissions and why

PermissionWhy
Accessibility serviceTo see which app comes to the front, so Stillwater can show its pause screen over the apps you picked during your hard hours. It reads nothing on the screen, stores no app history and sends nothing anywhere.

NotificationsYour two reminders (wind down, morning check-in). Their text never says what the app is for.
MicrophoneOnly when you tap Record to make a note to yourself. The file stays on the phone.
Alarms and start at bootTo check during the night whether protection is still on, and to restore the filter after a restart.

When information leaves your phone

  • Adult-site filter. When the filter is on, the names of websites your phone looks up (for example example.com, not the pages or anything you type) go to Cloudflare for Families (1.1.1.3), which answers that adult sites don't exist. Names on your allowed list go to Cloudflare's normal resolver (1.1.1.1) instead. Cloudflare's own privacy policy applies to these lookups.
  • Buying the 21-day Reset. Google Play handles the payment. To confirm the purchase on your phone, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives the purchase record from Google Play with a random app ID that is not your name or email. We see only that a purchase exists. Google's and RevenueCat's privacy policies apply.
  • What you send yourself. The ally code and the morning one-line report leave the phone only when you tap Send and pick an app (for example Messages). You see the full text first. It never contains apps, sites or searches.
  • Writing to us. If you email support@nisico.eu, we receive what you write and your email address, and use it only to answer you.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts. The launch email form sends your email address to Formspree, which forwards it to us; we use it once, to tell you when Stillwater is on Google Play, then delete the list.

Adults only

Stillwater is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect anything from children, and the app itself collects nothing about anyone.

Your rights

All app data is on your phone: you can delete it in the app (Settings › Faith, privacy & more › Delete all my data on this phone) or by uninstalling. For an email you sent us, or any question, write to support@nisico.eu; we answer within one month. EU and UK users can complain to their data protection authority; ours is the Romanian ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

Who we are

NISICO TECHNOLOGIES SRL, Romania (nisico.eu). Contact: support@nisico.eu. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the app shows the new version.

In crisis? Call or text 988 in the US (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call Samaritans on 116 123 in the UK. In danger now, call 911.

Stillwater is a self-help app for adults, not a medical or psychological treatment. It is not affiliated with Google, Apple or any church or organisation.

Stillwater support

A person reads every message. Write to support@nisico.eu and we reply within 2 days. You don't need to explain why you use the app.

In crisis? Call or text 988 in the US, or call Samaritans on 116 123 in the UK. In danger now, call 911.

Delete your data

Stillwater keeps everything on your phone and we have no copy, so you delete it yourself, at once:

  1. In the app: Settings › Faith, privacy & more › Delete all my data on this phone. This stops every pause and the filter, removes your reminders and your voice note, and clears everything else.
  2. Or uninstall the app. Android's cloud backup is off for Stillwater, so nothing comes back.

If you bought the 21-day Reset, the purchase stays with your Google Play account, so you can restore it later. Refunds go through Google Play.

Common questions

The pause screen didn't show

Open Stillwater. If Tonight says “Check needs attention”, tap Check setup: usually the Accessibility permission was turned off (Android does this after a Force stop). Then run Settings › Faith, privacy & more › Test my setup.

A normal website is blocked

Settings › Filter adult sites › Allowed sites. Add the site name. During your hard hours with Until morning on, the change waits until morning.

I need to end the night early

With Until morning on, changes that loosen protection wait until your hard hours end. If you set an ally code, ask your ally for it and use “End early with ally code”.

In crisis? Call or text 988 in the US (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call Samaritans on 116 123 in the UK. In danger now, call 911.

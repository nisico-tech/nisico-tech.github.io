Stillwater terms of use

Last updated: 16 November 2026

These terms cover the Stillwater app for Android, made by NISICO TECHNOLOGIES SRL, Romania (nisico.eu). The app shows the same points under Settings › Terms of use. By turning protection on, you accept them.

1. A self-help tool, not treatment

Stillwater is a self-help tool for adults. It is not medical or psychological treatment and not a crisis service. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the US, 112 in the EU, 999 in the UK). In the US you can call or text 988.

2. What protection can and cannot do

No app can lock a phone for good. A force stop, or turning off Accessibility or the VPN, stops the protection. The adult-site filter relies on Cloudflare for Families and does not catch every site. We don't promise that protection can't be stopped or that it blocks everything.

3. Your choice, your phone

You install Stillwater and turn it on by your own choice, on your own phone. You can turn it off or uninstall it at any time. Use it only on a phone that is yours.

4. Price and refunds

Protection and urge help are free. Only the 21-day Path costs a one-time price, shown in the app before you buy, paid through Google Play. It is not a subscription and never renews. Refunds follow Google Play's rules; you can also write to us. If you live in the EU or the UK, you agree that the Path opens at once after purchase, so the 14-day withdrawal right ends when the content starts to be supplied; this does not limit your other legal rights.

5. Your data

Everything you enter stays on your phone. See the privacy policy. Uninstalling deletes it.

6. Adults only

Stillwater is for people aged 18 and over.

7. As it is

The app is provided as it is. We keep fixing it; write to us when something doesn't work. To the extent the law allows, NISICO is not liable for indirect losses from using the app. Nothing here limits rights you have under the consumer law of the country where you live.

8. Changes and contact

If these terms change, the date at the top changes and the app shows the new version. Romanian law applies, without taking away the protection of your own country's consumer law. Questions: support@nisico.eu.

In crisis? Call or text 988 in the US (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call Samaritans on 116 123 in the UK. In danger now, call 911.

Can an app really block porn on an iPhone?

Partly, and we say so. Through Apple's Screen Time, Stillwater pauses the apps you pick and filters adult sites in Safari during your hours. You can always turn Screen Time off in Settings, so no app can honestly promise to be unbreakable.

Is it religious?

Not by default. If faith is one of your reasons, you can choose a faith path. It stays as private as everything else.

Does it shame me or reset my progress?

No. There are no streaks to lose and no countdowns. A hard night gets you a change for the next one, not a zero.

Holdline Support

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Contact

Write to support@nisico.eu. We aim to answer within 2 working days. Please never send health details, screenshots of your entries or doctor reports: we don't need them to help you.

Report a problem from the app

"Report a problem" (in Settings, under About) prepares a short message with only the app version and the name of the screen you were on. You see it in your phone's share sheet and send it yourself.

Common questions

  • Moving to a new phone: in Your data, save a backup file, move it to the new phone and restore it there. With Plus, use "Restore purchase" with the same store account.
  • Refunds: purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, so refunds are handled by the store under its rules.
  • Deleting your data: Settings > "Delete all data" erases everything on the phone. There is no account to delete. See https://nisico.eu/holdline/delete-data.html.
  • Turning off AI meal reading: use the "Read meals with AI" switch on the "What can leave this phone" screen (Account & plan > Privacy policy · data safety).

Complaints and security problems

  • Complaints: write to support@nisico.eu. We confirm we received it within 30 days and tell you what we did.
  • Security problems: if you find a security weakness in Holdline, write to support@nisico.eu with "Security" in the subject. We confirm within 3 working days and tell you when it is fixed.

If you need help now

Holdline cannot help in an emergency. Call your local emergency number. In the US and Canada you can call or text 988. In the UK and Ireland you can call Samaritans on 116 123.

Holdline Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This policy is for residents of Washington, Nevada and other US states with consumer health data laws, including the Washington My Health My Data Act and Nevada SB 370. It adds to the Holdline Privacy Policy (https://nisico.eu/holdline/privacy.html).

The short version

Almost everything you enter in Holdline stays on your phone, and we never receive it: weigh-ins, food, your treatment journal, rough days and symptoms, notes and reports. The only consumer health data we may collect is the meal sentence you choose to send for an AI reading.

What we collect

  • The meal sentence you say or type when you choose "Use AI to read it" (it may describe what you eat), with your phone's language setting.
  • A random install id and a one-way code made from your network address, used only to count readings per day.

Where it comes from

From you, only when you choose to use the AI meal reading.

Why we collect it

Only to read the meal and return foods, calories and protein, and to apply the daily limit of readings. We do not use it for anything else.

Who we share it with

  • Our hosting provider, Supabase (EU), which runs our meal service.
  • Anthropic, the provider of the AI model, which receives the sentence only, without the install id.

Both act only on our instructions. We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Our meal service does not store the sentence. Anthropic deletes it within 30 days (up to 2 years only if its safety systems flag a request) and does not use it to train models. The daily counters are deleted after 2 days.

Your consent

We collect the meal sentence only after you choose "Use AI to read it". You can withdraw consent at any time with the "Read meals with AI" switch on the "What can leave this phone" screen (Account & plan > Privacy policy · data safety); nothing is sent after that.

Your rights

You have the right to confirm whether we collect your consumer health data, to access it, to know who received it, to have it deleted and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store the sentence and the counters are deleted after 2 days, there is usually nothing left to give or delete. Write to support@nisico.eu; we answer within 45 days. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. If the appeal is declined, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General (www.atg.wa.gov) or the Nevada Attorney General (ag.nv.gov).

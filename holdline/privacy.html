Holdline Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

In short

  • Holdline has no account. There is no sign-up, no email and no name field.
  • Your health records (weigh-ins, food and protein, strength, treatment journal, rough days, notes, reports) are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • A few optional features use another service. They receive only what they need, and only when you use them: the meal sentence when you choose AI for meals, a barcode number when you scan, and your purchase, which your app store handles. Each one is listed below.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools. We do not sell or share your data.
  • Holdline is for adults 18 and over.

Who is responsible

The controller is NISICO TECHNOLOGIES SRL, Strada Stupca 22-32, Sector 6, 061042 Bucharest, Romania (company number J40/4720/16.03.2021, VAT RO43922705). For any privacy question or request, write to support@nisico.eu.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps what you enter in a database on your phone: your units and starting details, weight range, weigh-ins, food, protein and water entries, strength sessions, your treatment journal (medication, the dose as you typed it, injection site, side effects, notes), rough days and their symptoms, your status and plan, up to three care-team contacts you add, appointment notes, lesson progress and your settings. The database is not part of your phone's iCloud or Google backup. We have no copy of it, so we cannot see, recover or delete it for you: you control it in the app, where you can view, edit, export and delete it.

What leaves your phone, and who receives it

Reading a meal with AI (only after you choose "Use AI to read it")

What is sent: The sentence you say or type (up to 500 characters), your phone's language setting and a random install id

Who receives it: Our meal service, hosted by Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). It passes only the sentence, without the install id, to Claude, an AI model by Anthropic PBC (United States)

Why: To find the foods, calories and protein in the sentence

How long it is kept: Our meal service does not store the sentence; its logs hold only event names and counts. Anthropic does not use it to train models and deletes it within 30 days (longer only if its safety systems flag a request or the law requires it)

Daily limit of meal readings

What is sent: The random install id and a one-way code made from your network address

Who receives it: Our meal service

Why: To cap readings per phone and per network each day, so the service stays fair and is not misused

How long it is kept: Deleted after 2 days

Connection to our meal service

What is sent: Your phone's internet (IP) address, as with any website

Who receives it: Our meal service and Supabase

Why: Needed to deliver the answer

How long it is kept: Only in Supabase's short-lived network logs

Barcode lookup

What is sent: The barcode number and the app version

Who receives it: Open Food Facts, a non-profit food database (France), which also sees your phone's internet address

Why: To show the product's nutrition

How long it is kept: Under Open Food Facts' own terms

Purchases of Holdline Plus

What is sent: Your purchase (a one-time payment; nothing renews)

Who receives it: Apple App Store or Google Play

Why: To take the payment, restore it and handle refunds

How long it is kept: Under the store's terms; we never receive card details

Sharing and exports

What is sent: The file or text you choose (doctor report, backup file, health-record file, a short update)

Who receives it: The person or app you pick in your phone's share sheet

Why: Your own sharing

How long it is kept: Under the receiver's terms once shared

Speech is turned into text on your phone. Your voice never leaves it, and the recording is deleted when you save, discard or leave the meal. On a phone without on-device speech, Holdline does not offer the microphone. Nothing else leaves the phone.

Your choice about AI

The first time you use "Say what you ate", the app asks before anything is sent: "Use AI to read it" or "Type it myself". With "Type it myself" the meal is read on your phone and nothing is sent. You can change this at any time with the "Read meals with AI" switch on the "What can leave this phone" screen (Account & plan > Privacy policy · data safety); turning it off stops all sending at once. AI readings are estimates: check them before you save.

Doctor reports

A report is made on your phone and leaves it only when you export or share it, and only to where you send it. You can lock the file with a password. The password is used once to lock the file and is never stored or sent; tell it to your doctor separately.

Encrypted cloud backup (when available)

Cloud backup is not yet available in the app. When it is, it will be off unless you turn it on. Each backup is then locked on your phone with a key only you hold (your recovery code, AES-256-GCM) before it is stored by our hosting provider, Supabase in the EU. We cannot open it, reset the code or recover it: without your code nobody can open the backup, not even us. You can keep the code in your phone's own password manager, which we never see. Turning cloud backup off or using "Delete all data" deletes the stored copy. We will update this policy and the app's privacy screen before the feature is switched on.

Legal bases (EU, EEA and UK)

  • Reading a meal with AI and the daily limit: your explicit consent given in the app (GDPR and UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and, because a meal sentence can say something about your health, Art. 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw it with the switch; earlier readings are not affected.
  • Barcode lookup: you start it, and it is needed to give you the feature you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Purchases: the contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) and the stores' legal duties.
  • Storing data on your phone (ePrivacy Art. 5(3), PECR): only what is strictly needed for the features you use.

Processors and international transfers

Supabase hosts our meal service in the EU under its data processing terms. Anthropic processes the meal sentence in the United States under its Data Processing Addendum, which includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the UK Addendum. Neither may use your data for its own purposes.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting tools. We do not track you across apps or websites, do not use an advertising identifier, and do not sell or share personal data, including for targeted advertising.

Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada and other US states)

Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains, for US residents, which consumer health data we collect (only the meal sentence you choose to send), why, who receives it and how to use your rights. It is published at https://nisico.eu/holdline/health-data.html and in the app under Legal.

Deleting your data

Settings > "Delete all data" erases everything the app holds on your phone, including the install id and your AI choice. Uninstalling the app also removes it. The meal service's daily counters for the old id are deleted after 2 days. Copies you shared before are outside the app. See https://nisico.eu/holdline/delete-data.html.

Your rights (EU, EEA and UK)

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict and port your data, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time. For data on your phone, use the app's own controls. For anything else, write to support@nisico.eu; we answer within one month. You may complain to the Romanian authority ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro), to the authority in your own EU country, or in the UK to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Canada

We handle personal information under PIPEDA and, for Quebec residents, the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector. Our person in charge of the protection of personal information can be reached at support@nisico.eu. You can ask to access or correct your information and withdraw consent, and you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

Children

Holdline is for adults 18 and over. The app asks you to confirm "I'm 18 or older" when you set it up. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

Data on your phone is protected by your phone's own security: the app sandbox, the device lock and the optional app lock with Face ID, fingerprint or passcode (the app never receives biometric data). Everything sent uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) without cookies. Reminders are local notifications; there is no push service. If a security incident affects information we hold, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Not medical advice

Holdline is a personal tracking tool. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment guidance and does not promise any result. Talk to a qualified health professional about your health and treatment.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date, and important changes are shown in the app. If a change would send new data from your phone, the app asks you first.

Delete your Holdline data

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Holdline has no account, so there is no account to delete. Your records live on your phone.

Delete everything in the app

  1. Open Holdline and go to Settings.
  2. Tap "Delete all data" and confirm.

This erases every entry, setting, report file and the random install id on the phone at once. Uninstalling the app also removes everything.

What else is deleted, and when

  • The meal service's daily counters for your install id are deleted after 2 days.
  • Meal sentences you sent for an AI reading are not stored by us; Anthropic deletes them within 30 days.
  • Files you shared or exported earlier are outside the app; delete them where you saved or sent them.
  • Purchases are kept by your app store under its rules.

Emails

If you wrote to us at support@nisico.eu, ask us to delete your emails and we will do so within 30 days.

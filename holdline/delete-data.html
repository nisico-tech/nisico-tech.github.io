Delete your Holdline data

Holdline has no account. Everything you log is kept on your phone only, and we do not hold a copy.

Delete it in the app

Open Settings, then Delete all data. This erases everything the app holds on your phone straight away and cannot be undone. Export a CSV file first if you want to keep a copy. Deleting the Holdline app from your phone also removes its data.

Delete what you sent us

If you emailed us, write to support@nisico.eu with the subject “Delete my Holdline data”. We delete those messages within 30 days and confirm by email.

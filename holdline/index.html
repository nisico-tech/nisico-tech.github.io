What Holdline does

Dose log and level estimate

Log injections or tablets and rotate injection sites. An educational level estimate from your logged doses and label values, not a blood test. Reminders never name your medicine.

Protein and strength

Log protein by voice, barcode or a quick tap. On queasy days, easy foods that still count. A short home strength plan is free; Plus adds sessions that step up, gym and on-the-road plans. No calorie counting.

A summary for your next visit

One page with your doses, side effects and weight trend, ready to review with your prescriber.

Your history through every change

Paused, stopped, switched or back on: your records stay. Coverage ending? Add the date and a free checklist appears on Today.

How it works

  1. 1

    Import or start fresh

    Bring your log from a CSV export or Apple Health, or log today's dose.

  2. 2

    Log a few things a day

    A dose, a meal's protein, a short session. About a minute.

  3. 3

    Change status when life does

    Your history, trend and routine carry on. With Plus, a 2-minute weekly check lets you pick one next step for protein, strength or weigh-ins.

Questions

Is Holdline only for people stopping their medication?

No. Most people use it while on treatment: dose log, level estimate, protein and strength. The difference is that your history stays useful if you later pause, stop, switch or restart.

What is free and what is in Plus?

Free: dose log, estimated level, protein, home strength plan, the visit summary, import and export, your full history and a coverage-change checklist. Plus adds a 2-minute weekly check where you pick one next step about protein, strength or weigh-ins (never medication), and self-guided support for the year after a stop or pause.

How is the medication level estimated?

With a simple model of your logged doses and the half-life and timing values in each medicine's official label, shown relative to your own steady level. It ignores how your body differs from the average, missed or late doses you did not log, and food or other medicines. It is an educational estimate, not a blood test, not a treatment target and not a measure of how well the medicine works. Never use it to decide a dose.

Where is my health data kept?

On your phone. The free core needs no account, and your health data stays on your phone unless you share it, for example a visit summary or a backup file you save yourself. A barcode lookup sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts; purchases go through the App Store or Google Play. You can export or delete everything in the app.

Can I bring my data, and is it on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Holdline imports a CSV export, including from Shotsy, and can read weight from Apple Health. It comes to iPhone and Android at the same time and works with large text sizes.

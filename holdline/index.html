What Holdline does

Dose log and level estimate

Log injections or tablets, rotate injection sites, and see an estimated level built from the official label values. Reminders never name your medicine.

Protein and strength

Log protein by voice, barcode or a quick tap. On queasy days, easy foods that still count. A short home strength plan is free; Plus adds sessions that step up, gym and on-the-road plans. No calorie counting.

A summary for your next visit

One page with your doses, side effects and weight trend, ready to share with your prescriber or GP.

Your history through every change

Paused, stopped, switched or back on: nothing resets. Coverage ending? Add the date and a free checklist appears on Today.

How it works

  1. 1

    Import or start fresh

    Bring your log from a CSV export or Apple Health, or log today's dose.

  2. 2

    Log a few things a day

    A dose, a meal's protein, a short session. About a minute.

  3. 3

    Change status when life does

    Your history, trend and routine carry on. With Plus, a 2-minute weekly check suggests one next step.

Questions

Is Holdline only for people stopping their medication?

No. Most people use it while on treatment: dose log, level estimate, protein and strength. The difference is that your history stays useful if you later pause, stop, switch or restart.

What is free and what is in Plus?

Free: dose log, estimated level, protein, home strength plan, the visit summary, import and export, your full history and a coverage-change checklist. Plus adds a 2-minute weekly check with one next step you choose, and a programme for the year after stopping.

How is the medication level estimated?

From your logged doses and the half-life and timing values in each medicine's official label. It is an estimate for your own records, not a blood test, and it does not tell you what to take.

Can I bring my data from another tracker?

Yes. Holdline imports a CSV export, including from Shotsy, and can read weight from Apple Health. You can export everything at any time.

Will it be on iPhone and Android?

Yes, both at the same time, and it works with large text sizes. Join the waitlist and we will email you once when it is ready.

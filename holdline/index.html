What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • Not medical advice and no dosing guidance

  • No ads, no calorie shaming

Questions

Do I need to be stopping my medication?

No. Holdline works for every stage, including staying on a lower dose or starting again.

Does it tell me what dose to take?

No. Medication decisions belong to you and your prescriber.

What will be free?

The core plan, Plan B and the restart path are planned to stay free.

Holdline Data Safety

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This is the summary we give the App Store and Google Play. The Privacy Policy has the full details.

Kept only on your phone

Your weigh-ins, food and protein, strength sessions, treatment journal, rough days and symptoms, notes, care-team contacts and reports. We never receive them. They are not part of your phone's cloud backup.

Sent only when you use a feature

  • Meal sentence, when you choose "Use AI to read it": to our meal service (Supabase, EU) and Claude by Anthropic. Not linked to you, not used for tracking, not used to train models, deleted by Anthropic within 30 days.
  • Barcode number, when you scan: to Open Food Facts.
  • Purchase, when you buy Plus: handled by your app store.

Never

  • No account, no name, no email.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no crash reporter.
  • No sale or sharing of your data.

Security

  • Everything sent uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • You can lock the app with Face ID, fingerprint or passcode.
  • You can lock a doctor report with a password.

Your control

  • Turn off AI meal reading in Settings > Privacy.
  • Export everything as a backup file in Your data.
  • Delete everything with Settings > "Delete all data".

What the app is

Holdline is a personal log for weight, food and protein, strength, habits, a treatment journal, rough days and notes, with trends and a report you can share with your doctor. It keeps your records on your phone. Some optional features use outside services (AI meal reading, barcode lookup, store purchases); the Privacy Policy lists them.

AI meal reading

If you choose "Use AI to read it", the meal sentence you say or type is sent to our meal service and to an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) to estimate foods, calories and protein. The number of AI readings per day is limited (3 a day on the free plan, up to 30 with Plus) to keep the service fair. Results can be wrong: review every number before you save it. You can switch this off with the "Read meals with AI" switch on the "What can leave this phone" screen (Account & plan > Privacy policy · data safety) and type meals yourself.

What “private by default” means in our apps

NISICO studio · 2026-11-04

"Private" is easy to put on a website. This is what we mean by it, in practice.

Your data stays on your phone where it can

If an app can do its job without a server, it does. Routines, photos, logs and notes are stored on your device first. You do not need an account to start using our apps, and a child never needs one.

When something has to leave your phone, you choose it

Some features need a server: a backup, a family shared between two parents, a link you send to a grandparent, a problem report. Each one is something you turn on or send yourself, and each app's privacy policy lists what is sent, where it is stored and for how long. We use providers in the EU where we can.

What we never do

  • No ads, and no advertising or tracking tools inside our apps.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No profiling, and no dark patterns that push you to share more.

Your rights, without a fight

We are a company in Romania, in the EU, so the GDPR applies to everything we do. You can ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it, by writing to support@nisico.eu. Most of the time the answer is short, because we hold very little.

Each app has its own privacy policy with the exact details, for example Steply's.

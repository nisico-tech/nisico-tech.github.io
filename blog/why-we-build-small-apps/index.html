Why we build small apps

NISICO studio · 2026-11-04

Most app stores are full of large apps that try to serve everyone. That works for the average user. It works less well for a parent whose autistic child needs to see each step of the morning, a truck driver studying for an English test, or someone tracking a medicine through a pause in treatment. Their problem is real, but it is too small for a large company to build for.

That is where we start. Each NISICO app does one job for one group of people, and tries to do it better than a general app can.

How we choose what to build

  • A specific person with a specific problem. Not "productivity", but "a parent getting a 4-year-old out of the door by 7:30".
  • Proof that people want it. Every idea gets a page and a short waitlist before we build much. If nobody signs up, we stop.
  • A clear line on what it will not do. Each page says what the app is not, so nobody is disappointed later.

What small lets us do

A small app can be calm. It does not need ads, a feed or tricks to keep you scrolling, because it is paid for by the people who use it, not by advertisers. It can keep your data on your phone, because it does not need to learn from millions of users. And it can be finished: an app that does its one job well, instead of growing a new tab every month.

Our first app, Steply, is a visual schedule app for families of autistic children. You can see everything else we are working on in our apps.

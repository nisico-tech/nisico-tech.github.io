What Evenline does

Plan the evening

Decide before 6 pm, not at the fridge at 9.

Notice the moment

A 60-second pause when the urge for the next one comes.

A calm week view

Fewer drinks, more dry evenings, no red alarms.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. Your data stays on your phone.

What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • Not a treatment for alcohol dependence
  • No moralising, no streak shame
  • No account required

Questions

Do I have to quit?

No. Evenline is for cutting down, on your terms.

Is it anonymous?

There is no account; your entries stay on your device.

What if I think I am dependent?

Please talk to a doctor. Stopping suddenly can be unsafe for some people.

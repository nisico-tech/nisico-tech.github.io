We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

Questions

Do I need a watch?

It works best with an Apple Watch, Oura or similar, but you can log by hand.

Is it therapy?

No. It is a self-guided wellness program using techniques from CBT-I.

Is it safe for everyone?

No program is. A screening at the start turns off sleep restriction for people it may not suit.

What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • Not a substitute for a vet or a behaviourist
  • No punishment-based methods
  • Not a puppy course

Questions

Does it work for any adult dog?

It is built for adult and rescue dogs settling into a new home.

Is this training?

Partly. It is about trust and routine first, then the skills.

What if my dog shows aggression?

Settle will point you to a vet or qualified behaviourist; it does not replace them.

Questions

Is this the official USCIS app?

No. It is independent. Official study materials are free on uscis.gov.

Will it tell me how to answer my N-400 questions?

No. It explains what the questions mean; it never tells you what to answer. For legal questions, see an accredited representative or attorney.

Which languages?

Explanations in English, Spanish and French at first, more for the age exceptions.

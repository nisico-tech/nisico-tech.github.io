Delete your Pennylab data

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Pennylab has no account, so there is no account to delete. Your data lives on your phone.

Delete everything in the app

  1. Open Pennylab and go to Settings.
  2. Tap "Delete my progress" and confirm with "Delete everything".

This erases your numbers, lesson progress and settings on the phone at once. Uninstalling the app also removes them. A phone backup (iCloud or Google) can keep its own copy until you delete it there.

What else

  • We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.
  • Things you shared earlier are outside the app; delete them where you sent them.

Emails

If you wrote to us at support@nisico.eu, ask us to delete your emails and we will do so within 30 days.

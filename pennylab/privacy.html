Pennylab Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

In short

  • Pennylab has no account. There is no sign-up, no email and no name field.
  • What you enter (your paycheck, monthly costs, 401(k) choices, lesson progress and settings) is saved only on your phone. We never receive it.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools. We do not sell or share your data.
  • Pennylab is for adults 18 and over.

Who is responsible

The controller is NISICO TECHNOLOGIES SRL, Strada Stupca 22-32, Sector 6, 061042 Bucharest, Romania (company number J40/4720/16.03.2021, VAT RO43922705). For any privacy question or request, write to support@nisico.eu.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps what you enter in a file on your phone: your take-home pay and the amounts the app works out from it, your monthly costs, your 401(k) and match choices, the lessons you finished and your settings. We have no copy of it. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may copy that file to your account there, as it does for other apps; we never see it.

What leaves your phone, and who receives it

App updates

What is sent: The app version, the platform (Android or iOS) and an update channel name, plus your phone's internet (IP) address, as with any internet request. None of your numbers or progress.

Who receives it: Expo (650 Industries, Inc., United States), the service that delivers app updates

Why: To check whether a newer version of the app exists and download it

How long it is kept: Under Expo's own terms, in short-lived service logs

Sharing your plan

What is sent: Only the text or picture you choose to share. Amounts are hidden by default.

Who receives it: The person or app you pick in your phone's share sheet

Why: Your own sharing

How long it is kept: Under the receiver's terms once shared

Sources and links

What is sent: Your phone's internet address, as with any website

Who receives it: The website a source link opens in your browser

Why: You chose to open it

How long it is kept: Under that website's own policy

Nothing else leaves the phone.

Purchases

The current version of Pennylab sells nothing. If a paid pack is offered later, the app will show its price and terms before you buy, the purchase will be handled by your app store, and this policy will be updated first.

Legal bases (EU, EEA and UK)

Checking for updates: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and secure (GDPR and UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Storing data on your phone (ePrivacy Art. 5(3), PECR): only what is strictly needed for the features you use.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting tools. We do not track you across apps or websites, do not use an advertising identifier, and do not sell or share personal data, including for targeted advertising.

Deleting your data

Settings > "Delete my progress" erases everything the app holds on your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it, but a phone backup can keep its own copy until you delete it there. See https://nisico.eu/pennylab/delete-data.html.

Your rights (EU, EEA and UK)

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict and port your data, and to object. For data on your phone, use the app's own controls. For anything else, write to support@nisico.eu; we answer within one month. You may also complain to the Romanian authority ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro), to the authority in your own EU country, or in the UK to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

United States

We do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising. To ask about your rights under your state's law, write to support@nisico.eu.

Canada

We handle personal information under PIPEDA and, for Quebec residents, the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector. Our person in charge of the protection of personal information can be reached at support@nisico.eu.

Children

Pennylab is for adults 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

Data on your phone is protected by your phone's own security (the app sandbox and the device lock). Update checks use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Not financial advice

Pennylab is an education and practice tool. Results are estimates. It does not give financial, tax or legal advice and does not move real money.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. If a change would send new data from your phone, for example accounts, the app asks you first.

Questions

What does the estimate include?

Your pay before taxes is worked back from your paycheck. Taxes use 2026 federal income tax for a single filer plus Social Security and Medicare. State income tax is not included yet. Every estimate is marked, and the math is shown under the result.

Does it handle every employer match formula?

Not yet. Pennylab supports a simple dollar-for-dollar match up to a percentage of pay. For a 50% or tiered match, our guide on how a 401(k) match works shows the math.

Do I have to connect my bank or employer account?

No. You type a few numbers yourself, or start with sample numbers. Pennylab never connects to your bank, payroll or 401(k) account. It is built by NISICO, a small independent software studio; no bank, employer or plan provider is involved.

Does it recommend investments?

No. It shows what a 401(k) contribution does to your paycheck and match. It never recommends funds or securities.

Is it free?

The first decision is free. Deeper scenarios may come as an optional one-time purchase, never a subscription you forget about.

Common questions

Does Pennylab move real money or connect to my bank?

No. Everything is a simulation on your own numbers. Nothing connects to a bank, an employer or a 401(k) plan.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. Pennylab has no account. See the Privacy Policy.

Are the numbers exact?

No, they are estimates (2026 federal tax for a single filer and FICA; state tax is not included). Check your own pay stub and plan documents before you decide.

How do I start over?

Settings > "Delete my progress". See Delete your data.

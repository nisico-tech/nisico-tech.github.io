What Tinystart does

One tiny first step

Not a to-do list. Just the next move, small enough that starting feels silly to avoid.

Short, kind timers

Two, five or ten minutes. Stopping after is a win, not a failure.

No shame, no streaks

Missed a day? Nothing breaks. Come back when you can.

Private

Your tasks stay on your phone.

How it works

  1. 1

    Name the avoided thing

    Taxes, an email, the pile on the chair.

  2. 2

    Get one tiny step

    “Open the folder.” That is all.

  3. 3

    Start the timer

    Keep going if you want. Or stop, guilt-free.

What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • Not a diagnosis or treatment for ADHD
  • Not another productivity system
  • No streaks to break

Questions

Do I need an ADHD diagnosis?

No. It is built for adults with ADHD, but anyone who gets stuck starting can use it.

Is it a to-do list app?

No. It helps with the one task you are avoiding right now.

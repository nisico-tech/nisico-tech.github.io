What Rehearse does

Hold to talk, take your time

“I'm not done” and “Help me say it” are always there.

Check what we heard

Unclear words are not marked as mistakes. Feedback on the task first, then at most three fixes.

A pocket sheet

The phrases for your appointment, offline, on your phone.

Pflege pack

Ward, care home, handover and on-call scenarios for nurses moving from B1 to B2.

How it works

  1. 1

What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • No “fluent in 30 days” promises
  • No medical, legal or immigration advice
  • No recordings kept

Questions

Who is it for?

People living and working in Germany who have a specific conversation coming up, and nurses preparing for care work.

I recruit or employ nurses. Can we use it?

Yes. Choose “Organisation” on the waitlist and we will contact you about a pilot.

Is the app in German?

The practice is in German; the app itself is in English at first.

What Kinword does

Read the family chat

Paste a message, see the sound and the meaning, and learn from it.

Reply in the right tone

How you say it to your mum is not how you say it to a friend.

Read from what you know

Start from words you already use, not from “apple, banana”.

Family moments

A call with grandma, a wedding toast, a doctor visit with a parent.

How it works

  1. 1

    Listening check

    Skip the beginner lessons you do not need.

  2. 2

    Bring a real message

    From the family group chat.

  3. 3

    Reply yourself

    With help, then without.

What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • Not a from-zero course
  • Not an exam course
  • Not a translator that answers for you

Questions

Which languages?

Korean first, then Mandarin (simplified and traditional).

I do not know any characters. Is that ok?

Yes. You start from the language you already hear and speak.

Are my family messages stored?

They are processed to help you and not kept by default.

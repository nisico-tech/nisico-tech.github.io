What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • No promise to raise bone density
  • Does not replace your medication
  • Not a weight-loss app

Questions

Is it safe if I have had a fracture?

The safety check asks about fractures and falls and adjusts the plan; always check with your doctor first.

Do I need equipment?

No for level 1. Light dumbbells later.

Will this replace my medication?

No. It works alongside the care your doctor recommends.

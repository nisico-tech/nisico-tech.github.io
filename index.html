How we build

Four promises in every app

Private by default

Your data stays on your phone where it can. No account where none is needed. We never sell data.

No ads, no dark patterns

No ads, no fake timers, no streaks that punish you for living your life.

Honest pricing

A useful free core. If an app has a paid plan, you see the price first, and we email you 2 days before a trial turns into a charge.

Tested with real people

Every app is shaped with the people it is for, and says plainly what it will not do.

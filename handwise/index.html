What Handwise does

Camera feedback

Fingerspelling and approved signs, with a clear “sure / not sure”.

Taught by Deaf teachers

Lessons filmed and reviewed by Deaf teachers.

Reading practice

Watch real signers and choose what they said.

Video never leaves the phone

Recognition runs on the device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick your path

    Class, partner, parent or interpreter.

  2. 2

    Watch, then sign

    The camera checks what it can.

  3. 3

    Read it back

    Receptive practice at real speed.

What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • Does not translate ASL
  • Does not grade facial grammar
  • Does not replace a Deaf teacher or the community

Questions

Can the camera check every sign?

No. Only signs that passed a strict test with Deaf signers get camera checks; the rest are marked “teacher only”.

Is my video saved?

No. Recognition runs on your phone and nothing is stored or sent.

Who teaches?

Deaf teachers. We will name them when they are signed.

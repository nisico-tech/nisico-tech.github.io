What Roadside Ready does

Practise the interview out loud

A simulated officer asks the common questions. You answer, it helps you say it clearly.

Road signs and documents

Explain signs and talk about your CDL, medical card, ELD and bill of lading in plain English.

CDL test in English

Knowledge-test practice in English with explanations in your language.

Safe by design

Speaking practice only when parked. On the road: listening only.

How it works

  1. 1

    3-minute check

    See where your English is today.

  2. 2

    10 minutes a day

    Practise the questions, signs and phrases.

  3. 3

    Mock check

    Run a full practice interview when parked.

What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • Not for use during a real inspection
  • Not a live translator
  • No promise that you will pass

Questions

Which languages?

Spanish first, then Punjabi and Russian/Ukrainian. The practice itself is in English.

Can fleets use it?

Yes. A plan for small fleets is planned. Tell us on the waitlist that you run a fleet.

Can I use it while driving?

Only the listening mode. Speaking practice unlocks when the truck is parked.

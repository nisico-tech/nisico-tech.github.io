What Midform does

A plan that progresses

It adds weight or reps when today felt easy. You never have to guess.

10-second check-in

Slept badly or sore? Today's session gets lighter, same movements.

Protein, simply

A daily target in grams or palms. No calorie counting.

Bone-smart option

Impact off by default, built up gradually and only when it is right for you.

How it works

  1. 1

    Tell it your setup

    Home or gym, experience, days.

  2. 2

    Do today's session

    Each move shown with three cues.

  3. 3

    Watch strength grow

    From 8 lb to 15 lb, in your log.

What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • No HRT or medication advice
  • No promise to reduce hot flushes
  • Not a calorie diet

Questions

I have never lifted weights. Is it for me?

Yes. The Foundations plan starts from zero.

I have osteoporosis.

There is a bone-smart plan; check with your doctor or physical therapist before you start.

Does it track symptoms?

Only what changes your training today. It is not a symptom tracker.

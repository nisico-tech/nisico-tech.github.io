What Blendwise does

Decodable stories

Only the sounds your child has learned so far.

In the tutor's order

Sounds, blending, words, sentences, then a short story.

Parent as listener

Your child reads aloud; you tap “right” or “again” per word.

Weekly report

A PDF for the tutor, the school or an IEP meeting.

How it works

  1. 1

    5-minute placement

    No good or bad score shown to your child.

  2. 2

    Ten minutes a night

    Your child reads, you listen.

  3. 3

    Share the report

    With the tutor each week.

What it will not be

We would rather say it now than disappoint you later.

  • Does not diagnose dyslexia
  • Does not replace a tutor or an evaluation
  • No ads, no voice recordings

Questions

Is it for dyslexia?

It is for children who struggle to read, diagnosed or not. It does not diagnose.

Does it listen to my child?

No. You are the listener. No voice is recorded.

I am a tutor. Can I use it?

Yes. Choose “Tutor” on the waitlist; tutors are part of how we build it.

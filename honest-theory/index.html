Questions

Is it the official app?

No. It is an independent practice app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the DVSA.

Does it cover Northern Ireland?

No, Great Britain (England, Scotland and Wales) only.

Why “honest”?

Because a high score on questions you have memorised says little. We measure on fresh questions.
